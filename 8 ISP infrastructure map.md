| Layer                 | Device / Equipment                   | What it does                                                             | Typical physical location                    | Connects **from**             | Connects **to**                            |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 🏠 Customer           | **Wi-Fi Router**                     | Provides Wi-Fi/LAN, NAT, firewall                                        | Customer home                                | ONT                           | Laptop, phone, TV, etc.                    |
| 🏠 Customer           | **ONT / ONU**                        | Converts optical fiber ↔ Ethernet                                        | Customer home                                | Fiber from ISP                | Router                                     |
| 🛣️ Street            | **Fiber Distribution Box / FDB**     | Terminates/distributes customer fibers                                   | Pole, roadside box, building                 | ISP distribution fiber        | Customer drop fibers                       |
| 🛣️ Street / Building | **Optical Splitter**                 | Splits one PON fiber among many customers                                | FDB, cabinet, building telecom room          | Fiber from OLT                | Multiple customer fibers                   |
| 🛣️ Street            | **Fiber Distribution Cabinet / FDH** | Houses splitters + fiber termination/patching                            | Street-side cabinet                          | Feeder fiber                  | Distribution/drop fibers                   |
| 🏢 Local Facility     | **OLT**                              | Terminates PON; communicates with ONTs                                   | ISP exchange / access facility / data center | Aggregation network           | PON fiber → splitters                      |
| 🏢 Local Facility     | **Access Switch**                    | Aggregates Ethernet/fiber connections                                    | ISP PoP / exchange                           | OLTs / other access equipment | Aggregation router/switch                  |
| 🏢 ISP PoP            | **Aggregation Switch/Router**        | Combines traffic from many OLTs/access sites                             | ISP PoP / metro facility                     | Multiple OLT/access networks  | Metro/core routers                         |
| 🏢 ISP PoP            | **BNG / BRAS**                       | Terminates subscriber sessions, assigns IPs, applies subscriber policies | ISP PoP / data center                        | Aggregation network           | ISP core                                   |
| 🏢 ISP PoP            | **AAA / RADIUS**                     | Authenticates customers and provides subscriber information              | ISP data center                              | BNG                           | User database / billing systems            |
| 🏙️ Metro Facility    | **Metro Router**                     | Carries traffic between different areas of the city                      | Metro PoP                                    | Aggregation routers           | Core routers                               |
| 🏢 Core Facility      | **Core Router**                      | Moves enormous amounts of traffic around ISP network                     | Major ISP data center / core PoP             | Metro/BNG                     | Other core routers / edge                  |
| 🏢 Edge Facility      | **Border / Edge Router**             | Connects ISP to other networks                                           | ISP data center / Internet edge PoP          | ISP core                      | Transit providers / IXPs / peers           |
| 🏢 Internet Exchange  | **Peering Router**                   | Exchanges traffic directly with other networks                           | IXP facility                                 | ISP edge router               | Google, Meta, Cloudflare, other ISPs, etc. |
| 🌐 External           | **Transit Provider Router**          | Provides access to networks your ISP doesn't directly peer with          | Other carrier's network                      | Your ISP                      | Rest of Internet                           |
| 🏢 ISP DC / PoP       | **CGNAT**                            | Shares public IPv4 addresses among customers                             | ISP data center / core/edge                  | BNG                           | Internet                                   |
| 🏢 ISP DC / PoP       | **DNS Servers**                      | Converts domain names → IP addresses                                     | ISP data center                              | Customer/BNG                  | DNS hierarchy                              |
| 🏢 ISP DC / PoP       | **DHCP Server**                      | Gives IP/network configuration                                           | ISP data center or distributed               | BNG/network                   | Subscriber                                 |
| 🏢 ISP DC             | **CDN Cache**                        | Stores popular content close to customers                                | ISP data center / PoP                        | ISP network                   | Customers                                  |
| 🏢 ISP DC             | **Network Monitoring**               | Monitors links/devices/traffic                                           | NOC                                          | Network devices               | NOC operators                              |
| 🏢 ISP                | **NOC**                              | Humans monitor/control the entire network                                | Network Operations Center                    | Monitoring systems            | Engineers/devices                          |
